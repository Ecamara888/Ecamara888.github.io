## How I publish a new post

Each post is a file in the `_posts/` folder named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`. For example, for a week 1 post:

```
_posts/2026-10-25-week-01-processes.md
```

And at the top, this front matter:

```yaml
---
layout: post
title: "Week 1 — Processes"
date: 2026-10-25 10:00:00 +0100
categories: course
---
```

Below it I write in Markdown, and after `git push` it publishes automatically.
